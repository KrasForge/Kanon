# Contributing

Read AGENTS.md and docs/development.md. Keep engineering claims evidence-backed. Changes to
schemas require compatibility notes and valid/invalid fixtures. Every new check needs a
real implementation and failure-path tests; do not register a success-returning stub.
Run pytest and ruff check . before review. Describe validation limits and skipped checks.
Keep issue acceptance criteria open until demonstrated. Do not include proprietary
supplier models or datasheets without redistribution permission.
